# Creative review and revision policy

The review inbox uses the supplied reference's three-part hierarchy: improvement controls, actual saved media/copy, and destination/scheduling details. It retains Marketingbus styling. Narrow screens stack preview, feedback and delivery. Destination filtering stays above the canvas; a label never implies an account connection or a published post. Media is fetched privately and exact source versions are revalidated. Copy, artwork, layout and final-video approvals remain distinct.

Artwork feedback can request and explicitly authorize a quote in the canvas. Copy feedback uses the existing focused revision workflow. Video changes open the exact-scene editor rather than silently regenerating a complete video. Layout, branding and original media open the existing composition editor. No unsupported edit is represented as an AI capability.

## Three AI revision requests per creative

- A creative is one stable production item, shared across its copy, artwork and video scenes.
- Initial copy, initial artwork and each original video scene do not use a revision request. Original scene IDs are fixed by the storyboard at the first footage authorization; renaming or adding scenes after that cannot create an unlimited free-initial-take loophole.
- Authorizing changed copy, replacement artwork (even without a parent pointer), or a replacement take for the same scene consumes one request. Merely requesting feedback or preparing a quote consumes none.
- The limit is three requests total, not three for each model, media type, team member or version. Credit allowances and quoted costs are separate.
- Requests count when authorized, including subsequently cancelled or unsuccessful attempts. Financial refunds do not reset creative revision history. Uncertain jobs remain protected against resubmission.
- Manual copy/design edits, comments, rejection, approval and scheduling do not consume requests. They still obey existing permission, fact-checking and exact-version approval rules.
- The counter is server-owned, scoped to the tenant and business, and atomically updated with the wallet/supplier admission. Duplicate acceptance returns the existing job without consuming again. A failed acceptance transaction rolls back the counter and holds.
- Requoting, deleting output media or creating a new copy/storyboard version does not reset the counter. Migration carries historical authorizations forward; existing totals above three are retained and prevent new revisions, without cancelling already-authorized work.

This is request-based cost control, not a promise of agency-level creative quality or three successful outputs. Live external provider acceptance and the broader product release gates remain separate.
